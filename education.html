Education that opens doors

Seth Vidyalaya and our scholarship scheme help children from the community stay in school and go further than anyone in their family has before.

Education

School is where it all begins

For a child growing up in a resettlement colony, school is rarely a given. Fees, distance, a family that needs another earner — any one of these can end an education before it starts.

Prayatn’s education work removes those obstacles one at a time. We run a school in the community, support meritorious students who would otherwise drop out, and keep parents closely involved in their children’s learning through an active Parent Teacher Association.

Every child who finishes school changes what the next generation of their family believes is possible.

Projects

What this looks like on the ground

Seth Vidyalaya

Our school in the heart of the community, where children learn, play and are prepared for the world beyond the classroom.

A fifteen-member School Management Committee — parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and members from educational institutions — reviews the school’s progress and shapes its plan of action.

Scholarship Scheme for Meritorious Students

Bright students from families with very little are supported so that money never becomes the reason a promising child stops studying.
