How Prayatn is governed

Three bodies keep the organisation answerable — first of all to the community it serves.

Board of TrusteesSchool Management CommitteeParent Teacher AssociationTrust detailsTransparency
Board of Trustees

Seven trustees, meeting every quarter

The Board of Trustees, consisting of seven members, manages the affairs of Prayatn. A meeting is held every quarter to analyse the performance of the organisation — including the role and functioning of the faculty and staff — give suggestions, and monitor progress against the plan of action of each project.

In an organisation this size a quarterly review is not a formality. It is where difficulties get raised while they are still small.

School Management Committee

Fifteen members

Comprising parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and members from educational institutions. Three meetings were held to review the school’s progress and develop a plan of action in tune with it.

Parents bring what they see at home; teachers what they see in class; trustees carry responsibility for the whole; educationists bring a view from outside. Decisions taken by a group like that are harder to get wrong.

Parent Teacher Association

Parents and teachers together

A formal body of parents, teachers and management, formed to encourage parent participation in school activities. Its aim is to promote the all-round education of children and to update parents and teachers on the school’s activities and progress.

For parents who did not themselves finish school, being asked their opinion in a formal setting is part of what Prayatn is for.

Transparency

Ask us anything

Small organisations earn trust by being easy to check. You are welcome to ask for our annual report and audited accounts, to visit the school and the health centre, and to talk to the trustees.

The most useful thing you can do is come and see. Write or call and we will arrange a time.

Prayatn runs on people who decided to show up

An hour a week, a professional skill, a child’s school year, a box of books — there is a way for you to be part of this.
