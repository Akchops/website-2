How Prayatn is governed

Three bodies — a Board of Trustees, a School Management Committee and a Parent Teacher Association — keep our work transparent and accountable to the community.

Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees, consisting of seven members, manages the affairs of Prayatn. Meetings are held every quarter to analyse the performance of the organisation — including the role and functioning of the faculty and staff — to give suggestions, and to monitor progress and achievements in tune with the plan of action of each project.

School Management Committee

A fifteen-member School Management Committee has been constituted, comprising parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and members from educational institutions. Three School Management Committee meetings were held to review the progress of the school and develop a plan of action in tune with that progress.

Parent Teacher Association

Prayatn has a parent-teacher association — a formal organisation composed of parents, teachers and management — to encourage parent participation in school activities. The aim of the PTA is to promote the all-round education of children and to update parents and teachers on school activities and progress.
