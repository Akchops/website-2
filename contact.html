Visiting

The best way to understand Prayatn is to come and see it. We are glad to show visitors the school and the health centre — please call or write first so that someone is free to take you round, and so that we are not interrupting a class.

If you are a woman in need of support, or a parent seeking a place for your child, you can call the number above or come to the office during working hours.

We will only use your details to reply to you.
