Get Involved Donate

There is a place here for you

An hour a week, a professional skill, a child’s school year, or a box of books. Prayatn has always run on people who decided to show up.

Ways to help

Pick whichever fits your life

Give an hour a week

Teach, tutor, coach for exams, or help at a health check-up. Turning up regularly matters more than doing a great deal in one day.

Share a professional skill

Doctors, counsellors, teachers, women lawyers, vocational trainers, accountants. Skilled time given free goes a very long way here.

Support a student

Back a child through a year of school, or contribute to the scholarship scheme that keeps meritorious students studying.

Give materials

Books, stationery, uniforms, school bags, computers, teaching material, medicines and medical supplies. All used, not stored.

Partner with us

For companies and foundations: project funding, CSR partnerships, employee volunteering. You can see the whole of our work in one afternoon.

Intern with us

Students of social work, public health and education can spend a term on live projects rather than observing from the side.

What it is like

Coming back matters more than doing a lot

The most useful volunteer is not the one who does the most in a day. It is the one who returns next week, and the week after. Children respond to people who keep turning up.

You do not need experience in the development sector. You do need to be reliable, and to be comfortable in a neighbourhood that may be quite different from your own.
