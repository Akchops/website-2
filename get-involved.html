Volunteer

Coming back matters more than doing a lot

The most useful volunteer is not the one who does the most in a day. It is the one who returns next week, and the week after. Children respond to people who keep turning up.

There is work here for most people: teaching and tutoring, exam coaching, helping at health check-ups, running community sessions, and the ordinary admin that keeps a small organisation upright.

You do not need experience in the development sector. You do need to be reliable.

Professional skills

Skilled time given free goes a very long way here

Doctors & counsellors

For the health centre, the school health programme, and support to women.

Teachers

Particularly maths, science and English, at the stages where students struggle.

Women lawyers

To advise women on their rights and their options.

Vocational trainers

Tailoring, computers, beauty work, bookkeeping, spoken English.

Accountants

To help keep our reporting as clear as it should be.

Photographers & writers

To help us show this work honestly.

Partner with us

For companies, foundations and institutions

We already work with the Seth Foundation, Azim Premji Foundation, Samrat Offset Pvt Ltd, United Way of Delhi and Sleep Well Foundation. Each supports a particular part of the work.

A partnership here is unusually legible: the school, the health centre and the women’s programme are in one neighbourhood, all visitable in an afternoon. You can see exactly what your support does.

We are open to project funding, CSR partnerships, employee volunteering, and support in kind.

Support & give

Support a student, or give what is needed

You can back a child through a year of school, or contribute to the scholarship scheme that keeps meritorious students studying when families cannot carry the cost.

Gifts in kind are used, not stored: books, stationery, uniforms, school bags, computers, teaching material, medicines and medical supplies.

To discuss it, please write or call — we will tell you exactly what is needed at the moment.

Write to us

Tell us how you would like to help

Fill in the form and we will get back to you. If you would rather speak to someone, call 011-4568 8954 or write to prayatn_1994@yahoo.co.in.

We read every message. A reply usually takes a few working days — we are a small team.

We will only use your details to reply to you.
