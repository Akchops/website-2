There is a place here for you

Prayatn has always run on people who decided to show up. Whether you have an hour a week, a professional skill or the means to support a child’s education — we would love to hear from you.

Volunteer

Teach a class, help at a health camp, coach a student for exams, or lend a hand at community events. Regular volunteers make the biggest difference.

Share a skill

Doctors, counsellors, teachers, designers, accountants, photographers — professional time given free goes a very long way here.

Partner with us

Companies, foundations and institutions: we work with partners on education, health and women’s development programmes, including CSR initiatives.

Support a student

Back a child through a year of school, or contribute to the scholarship scheme for meritorious students.

Give in kind

Books, stationery, uniforms, computers, medicines and teaching material are always needed and always used.

Intern with us

Students of social work, public health and education can spend a term with us on live projects in the field.

Write to us

Tell us how you would like to help

Fill in the form and we will get back to you. If you prefer, call us on 011-4568 8954 or email prayatn_1994@yahoo.co.in directly.

We read every message. A reply usually takes a few working days.
