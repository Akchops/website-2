A developmental effort, since 1992

Prayatn is a registered Public Charitable Trust working for the disadvantaged sections of society — building capabilities, providing education and offering skill development opportunities for a dignified and independent life.

Our story

It started with a room and a few children

Prayatn began in 1992 with 21 children, in a makeshift arrangement in a two-room structure in a cluster near Nehru Place, New Delhi. There was no building, no funding and no certainty — only the conviction that no child should grow up without the chance to learn.

Three decades later, that conviction has grown into a school, a community health centre, a scholarship programme and a support system for women survivors of violence. What has not changed is the way we work: with the community, at its pace, on the problems it names itself.

Prayatn is registered with the Registrar of Societies as a Public Charitable Trust and is governed by a Board of seven trustees.

What guides us

Mission, vision and values

Our vision

A society in which everyone — regardless of income, caste or gender — is an equal and active partner in the journey of development.

Our mission

To uplift the disadvantaged sections of society by building capabilities, providing education and offering skill development opportunities that lead to a dignified and independent life.

Our approach

We work with communities rather than for them — parents, teachers, trustees and residents all have a seat at the table where decisions get made.

Our journey

Milestones along the way

  • 1992 — The beginning

    Prayatn is founded and registered as a Public Charitable Trust, starting with 21 children in a two-room structure.

  • Education takes root

    Seth Vidyalaya grows into a full school with a School Management Committee and an active Parent Teacher Association.

  • Health comes to the doorstep

    Swaasthya Kendra opens and the School Health Program begins reaching children where they study.

  • Standing with women

    Comprehensive care and support begins for women survivors of gender based violence in the community.

  • Today

    Five ongoing projects across education, healthcare and women development, run alongside partners who share our belief.

Governance

Accountable, by design

Three bodies keep our work transparent and answerable — to the community first of all.

Board of Trustees

Seven members who review the organisation’s performance every quarter and monitor progress against each project’s plan of action.

School Management Committee

Fifteen members — parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and educationists — who review the school’s progress and set its plan of action.

Parent Teacher Association

Parents, teachers and management together, encouraging parent participation and keeping everyone informed about school activities.
