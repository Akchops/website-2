Our story

Thirty years in one place

Prayatn was founded in 1992 to work with the disadvantaged sections of society, and it has been at that work ever since, from an office in Kalkaji, South Delhi.

Organisations that stay this long in one neighbourhood end up with something that cannot be built quickly: people know them. Families who came as children now send their own children.

What we believe

Partners, not beneficiaries

Everyone should be an equal and active partner in their own development. It sounds like a slogan until you notice how much charitable work quietly assumes the opposite.

Capability matters more than relief. Giving a family what they need today changes nothing on its own. Education, skills and health are what let a household stop depending on anyone’s goodwill — including ours.

Staying matters. Development does not happen in project cycles. It happens over the fifteen years it takes a child to grow up.

How we work

Six things that shape everything

Inside the community, not at a distance

Our office, school and health centre are in the same neighbourhood as the families we serve. People can walk in.

The people affected sit on the committees

Parents’ representatives help govern the school through the School Management Committee and the Parent Teacher Association.

We follow through rather than refer on

A check-up that finds a problem only helps if somebody makes sure the child is treated. Following through is most of the work.

Every project has a plan, and it is reviewed

The trustees meet each quarter to analyse performance and monitor progress against each project’s plan of action.

Health and education are one problem

A child who cannot see the blackboard is not a slow learner. Running both programmes lets us notice the connection.

Small enough to know people by name

We have never tried to scale into something that measures its work only in numbers.

Where we work

Kalkaji, South Delhi

We work in and around Kalkaji, from an office at E-103, Ground Floor, Kalkaji, New Delhi – 110019. It is a part of the city where considerable wealth and considerable hardship sit a few hundred metres apart.

After thirty years here, we are not guessing about what these families need — and they are not guessing about whether we will still be here next year.

Visitors are welcome — do call ahead.

Governance

Who is accountable, and to whom

Three bodies keep the organisation answerable — first of all to the community it serves.

Board of Trustees

Seven members manage the affairs of Prayatn. A meeting is held every quarter to analyse the organisation’s performance — including the role and functioning of the faculty and staff — give suggestions, and monitor progress against the plan of action of each project.

School Management Committee

Fifteen members: parents’ representatives, teachers, trustees and members from educational institutions. Three meetings were held to review the school’s progress and develop a plan of action in tune with it.

Parent Teacher Association

Parents, teachers and management together, encouraging parent participation in school activities and keeping everyone updated on the school’s activities and progress.
