A developmental effort

Three decades of standing with the community

Prayatn began in 1992 as a small effort to bring school within reach of children who had none. Three decades later, we work across education, healthcare and women’s development in and around Kalkaji, New Delhi — always alongside the community, never on its behalf.

As a registered Public Charitable Trust, we strive to build capabilities, provide education and offer skill development opportunities, ensuring a dignified and independent life for all. We want a society where everyone is an equal and active partner in the journey of development.
