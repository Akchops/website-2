Dignity, safety and a voice

Comprehensive care and support for women survivors of gender based violence — and the community work that helps prevent it.

Women development

Standing with women, for as long as it takes

Gender based violence rarely ends with a single incident, and getting free of it is rarely a single decision. It takes information, support, and someone who stays.

Prayatn provides comprehensive care and support to women survivors in the communities we work in — listening first, then helping with the practical steps: counselling, guidance, and accompaniment through the systems that are meant to protect them.

Projects

What this looks like on the ground

Addressing Gender Based Violence in Communities

Comprehensive care and support to women survivors of violence, delivered through the community rather than at a distance from it.

The work combines direct support for survivors with awareness and prevention efforts, so that fewer women need it in the first place.

Skills and self-reliance

Skill development opportunities that help women earn, decide and stand on their own — because independence is what makes safety last.

The difference it makes

Why it matters

Someone to turn to

A first point of contact that is close by, familiar and safe to approach.

Practical help

Support through counselling, paperwork and the institutions survivors must deal with.

Independence

Skills and earning ability, so that leaving is a real option.
