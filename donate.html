However you would like to help

Where it goes

What your giving pays for

Prayatn is small, and money given here is visible. It buys the ordinary things that keep the work standing: teachers and teaching material, medicines and a doctor’s time, and the counselling and accompaniment that a woman leaving a violent home needs.

You are welcome to come and see any of it. Ask for the annual report and the audited accounts, visit the school, talk to the trustees.

Tax relief

Claiming 80G

Donations to Prayatn may qualify for tax relief under section 80G of the Income Tax Act. Please write to us for the details you need for your claim.

Other ways

Giving that is not money

Books, stationery, uniforms, school bags, computers, teaching material, medicines and medical supplies are all used rather than stored.

So is time. Teachers, doctors, counsellors, lawyers and trainers who can give a few regular hours are worth a great deal to an organisation this size. See the ways to get involved.
