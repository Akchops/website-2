Get Involved

Where your giving goes

Prayatn is small, and money given here is visible. It keeps a school open, a health centre running, and a door open for women who need one.

What it pays for

Ordinary things, kept going year after year

There is nothing dramatic about what donations buy here. Teachers and teaching material. A doctor’s time and the medicines to hand out afterwards. Uniforms and school bags. The counselling and the accompaniment that a woman leaving a violent home needs across many months.

Those ordinary things are exactly what a family living close to the edge cannot reach on their own, and they are what change a life over fifteen years rather than fifteen minutes.

What it keeps running

Three things your giving holds up

Every rupee goes into one of these. They are all within a few streets of each other, and you are welcome to come and see any of them.

A school stays open

Seth Vidyalaya teaches children from the community, many of them the first in their family to attend school at all. Giving pays teachers, and puts books and uniforms in children’s hands.

A family can see a doctor

Swaasthya Kendra means everyday care is a short walk away rather than a lost day’s wages. Giving pays for a doctor’s time, medicines, and check-ups for school children.

A woman has somewhere to turn

Support for women survivors of violence takes months, not one appointment. Giving pays for counselling, for someone to go with her to a police station or a court, and for the skills that make leaving possible.

Why it reaches people

Small enough that nothing gets lost on the way

Prayatn has worked in the same neighbourhoods since 1992, from an office in the middle of them. There is no chain of offices between a donation and the child it pays for.

The trustees meet every quarter to check each project against its plan. Parents sit on the committee that runs the school. If you want to know where your money went, there is someone who can tell you, by name.

Ask for the annual report and the audited accounts. Better still, come and visit.

Donations to Prayatn may qualify for tax relief under section 80G of the Income Tax Act. Please write to us for the details you need for your claim.
