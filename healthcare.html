Healthcare within reach

Healthcare is fundamentally important for individual well-being and societal prosperity — ensuring longer, more fulfilling lives and a productive workforce.

Healthcare services

Care that does not require a long journey

When the nearest affordable clinic is an hour away, small illnesses become big ones. People wait, borrow, and often go without.

Prayatn runs a community health centre so that everyday care — a check-up, a prescription, advice a family can trust — is available close to home. Our School Health Program takes that same care into classrooms, catching problems early in the children who are least likely to be taken to a doctor.

Projects

What this looks like on the ground

Swaasthya Kendra

Our community health centre, offering everyday primary care close to home and within reach of every family in the neighbourhood.

School Health Program

Regular health check-ups, awareness sessions and follow-up care for school children, so that illness never becomes a reason to drop out.

Children who need further treatment are guided and accompanied to the care they need.

Why it matters

Early, not late

Close to home

Healthy enough to learn

Children who are well come to school — and stay in it.
